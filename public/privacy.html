Privacy Policy

Trip Visualizer · Last updated: 13 September 2026

This policy describes how Trip Visualizer (trip-visualizer-eta.vercel.app) handles information when you use the app, including optional Google Drive sync.

What the app stores

  • Trip plans (schedule, places, notes, costs) are stored mainly in your browser (IndexedDB / local storage) on your device.
  • Optional settings you enter in the app (for example a Maps key override) stay on your device unless you clear site data.
  • Nearby Explore results may be cached on your device for a short time to reduce repeat network calls.

Google Sign-In and Google Drive

If you use Save to Google Drive / load from Drive, you sign in with Google and grant access limited to files this app creates or opens (drive.file scope).

  • Workbooks are written under a folder named trip-planer in your Google Drive.
  • We do not receive or store your Google password. Access uses Google’s OAuth tokens in the browser session.
  • You can revoke access anytime in your Google Account permissions.

Maps and place data

The app may call map/place services (for example Google Places via our server proxy, OpenStreetMap / Nominatim, routing, and imagery providers) to show maps and nearby places. Those providers process the coordinates or queries needed to answer the request under their own policies.

Analytics and ads

We do not sell your personal data. The app is not monetized with ad networks.

Hosting

The web app is hosted on Vercel. Server logs from hosting/API routes may include standard technical data (such as IP address, user agent, and timestamps) as part of operating the service.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact method associated with the Google Cloud project / OAuth consent screen for this app, or the site operator who shared the app with you.
